# ADR-HEARTH-002: App name and logo

**Date:** 2026-09-08
**Status:** Accepted

## Context

Sean asked, mid-session: "create a name for it and logo for the app." This is
a creative/product decision delegated to Claude directly (not a request to
be asked back), so per ADR-GLOBAL-002 the decision is made and logged here
rather than blocking on a question.

## Decision

**Name: Hearth**

**Tagline:** "One home. One remote."

**Mark:** A roofline (chevron) over a glowing ember/sphere, warm
amber-to-gold gradient, on a dark navy rounded-square background.
Source vectors live in `assets/brand/*.svg`; rasterized app icons are
generated by `scripts/generate-app-icons.js` (uses `sharp`) into `assets/`.

**Palette:**
- Background navy: `#12141C` → `#1B2030` (gradient)
- Accent warm: `#FFC773` → `#FF7A45` (gradient), ember highlight `#FFE7B8`

## Rationale

- "Hearth" maps directly to the product vision: a single warm, central
  point that ties together every device in the home, echoing the literal
  historical hearth as the center of a household — a stronger metaphor than
  generic names like "Remote" or "Hub" and not already an overused app name.
- The roofline (home) + ember (fire/energy/"on" state) mark reads clearly at
  both large (App Store) and small (home-screen icon, 40px) sizes because it
  uses only two simple flat/gradient shapes — no fine detail that would be
  lost at small sizes.
- Keeping the brand mark as versioned SVG source + a regenerable script
  (rather than hand-exported PNGs only) means the logo can be refined later
  without losing the ability to regenerate every required icon variant
  (iOS icon, Android adaptive icon foreground/background/monochrome,
  favicon, splash) consistently.

## Consequences

- Any future logo revision should edit `assets/brand/icon-*.svg` and rerun
  `node scripts/generate-app-icons.js` rather than editing the PNGs in
  `assets/` directly (those are generated output).
- `bundleIdentifier` / `package` in `app.json` use `com.hearth.app` as a
  placeholder pending Sean's actual developer account / domain.
